**Title:** Odoo ERP Assistant (MCP)

**Short description (1 line):**
Let your AI agent read and work with Odoo safely: customers, stock, sales reports, overdue invoices and human-confirmed quotations.

**Long description:**
Odoo ERP Assistant connects Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot and other MCP-capable agents to your Odoo (14-18) through
odoo-mcp, an open-source MCP server, and teaches the agent how to use it well.

- **Business tools, not raw SQL:** find customers, check stock (with a low/out reorder list), sales reports by
  month/customer/product, and overdue invoices sorted by urgency.
- **Safe by default:** read-only unless you enable writes; model and field allow-lists; credentials and tokens can never
  be read; domains are validated.
- **Human in the loop:** quotations are created as drafts and only after you confirm the summary in your client.
- **Ready-made workflows:** month-end review, "who should we chase this week", and quote-from-a-chat-message.
- **Try it without an ERP:** a built-in demo company works out of the box.
